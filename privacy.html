Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Family Scheduler is a personal tool used only by the members of one household, with their own Google accounts.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read only): searches for scheduling emails such as appointments, auditions, rehearsals and show information, and reads their attachments.
  • Gmail (send): sends the family a daily schedule summary from the household's own account.
  • Google Calendar: reads events and adds events to the family's calendar.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to keep the family calendar up to date and to send the daily summary. Sign-in tokens and logs are stored only on the family's own computer. Saved email attachments are deleted after 14 days.

To find events in scheduling emails, their text and attachments are processed by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on the family's behalf. Otherwise, data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the family's own scheduling.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
